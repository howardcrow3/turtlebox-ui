# Changelog

## 0.4.0 (draft)
- Web layer for all websites (`css/web.css`, `docs/WEB.md`): the app tokens and controls at website scale, light mode only.
- `css/tokens-light.css` and `web` tokens (spacing, control heights, measure) in `tokens.json`.
- Inter 400, 500 and 600 in `fonts/`, and `dist/web-light.css` built by `scripts/build-web.py`.
- Adopted by highturtle.com and turtlebox.studio.

## 0.3.0 (draft)
- CeeVee adopted steps 5 to 9 (Jobs list and detail, Applications board, Sign in with Google, chat home, chat pane). Notes are in `docs/DESIGN.md`.
- `docs/GOLDEN.md`: how Golden consumes the framework.
- CeeVee adopted steps 1 to 4 of the plan (tokens and base, sidebar and account menu, settings modal with tabs, Job types). Notes from the build are in `docs/DESIGN.md`, section Adopted in CeeVee.
- `css/shell.css` (generated by `scripts/build-shell.py`): app shell only.
- Settings modal pieces in `css/extensions.css` (prefixed).
- Chat pieces in `css/extensions.css`: thread, bubbles, composer, suggestion chips (prefixed to avoid clashes).
- `css/extensions.css` and `docs/EXTENSIONS.md`: segmented control, field, notice, badge, empty state, progress, data table, section. Added for Golden, which now vendors the framework.
